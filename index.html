What is VentureVerse?

VentureVerse is a one-day event for students who are curious about starting something of their own. You do not need a business plan or any experience. If you have noticed a problem on campus or have a half-formed idea, this is a good place to bring it.

The day mixes talks with a hands-on sprint and a short pitching session, with enough breaks to meet people from other branches and years.

Why come along

  • See how an idea becomes a startup

    A walk through the early steps: spotting a problem, testing it, and finding your first users.

  • Hear a founder's story

    A guest founder talks about their journey, including the parts that did not go to plan.

  • Try an idea sprint

    Work in a small team on a campus problem and turn it into an idea you can explain in a few minutes.

  • Meet other students

    Find people who also like building things, and who might become teammates later.
